Understanding Siemens QA reports

Research checked November 9, 2026. Plain-language guidance for organizing documents, not instructions for performing scanner testing.

The key distinction: a report can identify a test without proving that the department's complete Monthly QA checklist has been satisfied. The application tracks saved documentation and reviewed labels; the department's approved procedure and current scanner instructions remain authoritative.

What the Siemens sources establish

The Siemens Academy report-review job aid describes Daily QA as including water homogeneity and noise, with PDFs stored under the scanner's SiteData\ConstancyReports directory. It shows a QualityDaily label. The document is dated August 2016 and explicitly refers users to the applicable operator instructions. The app's hospital UNC locations are editable; they are not assumed to be identical on every system. Siemens Academy source.

The SOMATOM Force syngo CT VA50A instructions, document 221330, describe daily measurements and an extended constancy process. Pages 394–395 identify light-marker positioning, topogram positioning, slice, homogeneity/water, noise, MTF, table positioning and CTDI-Air. Pages 421–423 discuss archived daily and monthly results and a generic Quality_Constancy_DATE.pdf filename. A generic Constancy name therefore cannot by itself prove Monthly classification or a complete test set. These are version-specific instructions, not proof of the hospital's installed software. Official Force instructions.

The SOMATOM Definition Edge syngo CT VB20 instructions, document 609971, describe daily water/CT-value, homogeneity and noise measurements at page 611, extended constancy at pages 616–617, and daily/monthly archived reports with generic Constancy PDF naming at pages 645–646. The current hospital manual and selected protocols must be checked before adopting a test schedule. Official Definition Edge instructions.

The Somaris 7 maintenance job aid names the Edge, Flash and Force product families and emphasizes system-specific instructions and service guidance. It supports the general terminology used here, but does not prove that every version has identical tests or PDF formatting. MDP Building remains a location label; this application does not assume its scanner is a Force. Siemens Somaris 7 source.

Why “Monthly” is department configured

ACR's CT QC support page, revised March 21, 2025, separates routine Daily checks, Monthly visual/display checks, and the annual qualified-medical-physicist evaluation. These categories do not map automatically to a Siemens scanner's exported PDF names. Some required activities can be documented outside the scanner's PDF folder. ACR CT quality-control guidance.

ACR's manual explanation, revised February 24, 2025, states that manufacturer-specific procedures, phantoms, specifications and frequencies matter; its manual does not replace applicable vendor conditions. It also explains changes for modern multidetector systems. This is why the app does not install a universal Flash/Edge/Force Monthly checklist or numerical pass/fail tolerances. ACR manual explanation.

One PDF or several PDFs can cover the same month

An authorized department user enters the required Monthly items in Scanner Connections, using the approved hospital checklist. During Check report label, the reviewer selects only the items verified in that exact PDF. One combined report can confirm several items; separate reports can contribute different items. The application combines their reviewed items by scanner and month.

A month can be marked documentation-complete only when every configured item has reviewed evidence. A report labeled Monthly without confirmed item details does not automatically satisfy the entire checklist. With no checklist configured, the application explains that completeness has not been assessed. Changing a PDF creates a separate report revision requiring its own confirmation.

How the reader and alerts behave

  • Filename rules, the 70/85/85 KiB size indicators and readable-content hints help identify possible categories. Size is never confirmation.
  • The optional portable reader extracts existing PDF text locally and lists found test words. It does not execute PDF scripts, send data to AI services, perform OCR, or change a category.
  • Found words do not establish passed results. Any reference/baseline wording should be checked in context.
  • The attention list distinguishes unavailable connections, absent saved records, unconfirmed report labels, and incomplete reviewed checklist evidence.
  • A missing PDF or unconfirmed checklist item is not a finding that clinical QA was omitted or that the scanner is noncompliant.
The original Siemens manuals are linked, not redistributed. Verify the exact model, installed syngo version, applicable instructions and department/QMP-approved procedure before configuring required items. The app is a document organizer and evidence tracker, not a replacement for medical-physics review.
