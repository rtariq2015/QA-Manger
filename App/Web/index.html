A simpler daily routine

  1. Update reports. Accessible scanners are checked and new PDFs are saved locally.
  2. Review the pending files. Approve keeps the suggested type. Disapprove keeps the PDF as another report. Either choice opens the next pending PDF. Undo is available.
  3. Find or share. Filter Reports, open the readable folders, or export the matching PDFs.

Report sorting is separate from clinical test interpretation and departmental checklist sign-off.

Keep the application and archive together when moving them. Windows-encrypted scanner sign-ins may need re-entry on a different PC or account.
