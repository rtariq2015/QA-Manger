Your QA reports, organized.

A local workspace for Daily reports, Monthly documents and easy sharing.

A simple routine: Update reports, check Today and Monthly, then handle any file exceptions in Review. Completed readings stay cached while you continue working.

Start the program

Extract the complete portable ZIP and double-click Start CT QA Manager.cmd. The app opens in your installed browser and runs on this computer. The helper and PDF readers are included; no separate runtime installation or administrator launch is needed. Your workstation policy must allow the included program and its local browser connection.

Display and finding reports

In Settings → Display & reading, choose Comfortable or Compact display density. Comfortable uses larger labels and controls; Compact fits more information. The choice applies to this local browser session and may reset when the app opens on a new local port. Light/Dark appearance remains separate. Smaller windows allow the Review controls and PDF to stack and scroll.

Reports shows up to 100 matching reports per page. Use Previous/Next, or the list’s arrow keys, Home and End. Search waits briefly while you type. The matching count covers all pages, and Exports includes every matching report, including reports beyond the preview or current page. A File label confirmed status describes organization, not clinical approval.

Monthly: set up once, then check the documents

Choose a month and scanner. Expand Checklist & due date to choose the tests your department requires, the due day and the month the checklist takes effect. Save the setup. Checklist versions preserve earlier months; changes apply from the effective month shown in the form.

Reports received counts filed Monthly PDFs. Items found shows which required test sections were recognized in their cached contents. Open a source report to inspect it. Unread, uncertain and unmatched custom checklist items remain visible. Printed warnings stay warnings, even when a test section is present.

Document receipt and detected sections describe the saved paperwork. They do not create clinical approval. Existing reviewed evidence is available separately under Previously reviewed evidence; the original Windows tools remain available for authorized evidence review.

Find a report or a day

Reports filters by scanner, year, month, type and search text. Switch to the Daily receipt calendar for saved Daily reports by date. It follows scanner schedules, activation dates and exclusions; future dates stay neutral. Selecting a day applies its exact Daily scope to the library and matching export. Clear the date filter to return to a broader search.

Review: one place to make the decision

Open a PDF and scroll through its pages. The left decision area says whether it applies to the Current PDF or Selected reports. Choose Daily, Monthly or Other when needed, then approve the displayed target. Disapprove keeps the PDF as Other. Selecting a checkbox also previews that report; opening an unselected report clears the batch with a notice.

Select up to 50 reports for a group decision. Keep each supported suggestion or choose one explicit file type. The selection is checked before saving; a changed member stops the batch. Undo and Undo batch restore their respective file labels. Expand Report details for cached contents and the Read contents button. Filing changes preserve original PDFs and existing clinical evidence.

Trends and background reading

Choose a scanner, measurement, test context and period. Supported measurements include water CT, image noise, slice thickness, light marker, external light marker, topogram position, sagittal/coronal alignment and table position. Table-position readings keep continuous and stepwise movement separate for each of the four printed positions. Units, printed limits and source pages stay attached to their values.

A PDF can be read successfully without containing the selected measurement. Coverage explains how many PDFs have cached readings and how many still need reading; chart counts describe only the chosen context. Click a point or table source to inspect its PDF page. Opening a chart does not read PDFs automatically.

Automatic reading works one PDF at a time while the app is open. Use Pause, Resume or Stop when needed. Stop prevents automatic restarts for the current session. Settings → Reading & filing offers a whole-library backlog and individual reading options. Supported results stay cached; unsupported tables can still need inspection.

Share reports and browse ordinary folders

Exports packages the reports matching its filters. Folders opens the complete readable Report Library: scanner → year → month → Daily / Monthly / Other. Managers can browse these PDFs without the program. Under More options, export the full library with an offline HTML summary and CSV, or create a full backup. Full-library export leaves filtered Exports selections intact.

A readable library is a convenient copy, while a full backup includes the archive and its saved records. Store backups on approved separate storage. Restore uses a new folder and leaves the current archive in place.

Connections, appearance and support

Settings → Scanner connections saves the report folder and sign-in for each scanner. A connection profile can supply paths and usernames; passwords are entered securely on the work computer. Remembered sign-ins are tied to the Windows account and may need re-entry after moving computers or accounts. Existing drive mappings are preserved.

Switch Light or Dark appearance using the header button. Scanner accent colors remain consistent across the views. Settings → Backup & support offers Export diagnostic ZIP and Open printable report; use Ctrl+P in the opened report to print or save it as PDF. Share the scrubbed support ZIP for troubleshooting. It contains retained structured actions, outcomes and counts, with no PDFs, extracted text, filenames, passwords or private paths. Nothing uploads automatically.

Update from a ZIP

In versions 5.3 and later, open Settings → Program updates, choose or drop an approved Update ZIP, check the version, then choose Install & restart. Do not unpack that ZIP or manually copy its files over your installation. The update preserves settings and reports and keeps a previous-program backup. Versions 5.2 and earlier use the separate one-time Upgrade ZIP.

Use Close local app in Settings when finished. Keep the program and its selected archive together when moving your workspace. Saved files remain available offline; checking scanner shares requires access to the workplace network.
