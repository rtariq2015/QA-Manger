  • Local destination permissions, real process termination during a synthetic transfer, restart recovery, and a copy of the existing archive.

Operational limits

Archive destinations must be direct local disk or USB folders. Network/mapped destinations and junctions are rejected to prevent writes through a scanner-drive alias. Scanner sources remain UNC paths. Fast updates use metadata; a source edit that preserves size and timestamps is not detected by the fast cache. The full audit compares saved PDFs against recorded hashes and does not reread unchanged scanner PDFs.

PDF inspection checks the header, closing marker, and bounded readable text/page hints. It does not render the full PDF or clinically validate the performed tests. Monthly confirmation requires an identified authorized reviewer and reason. Reviewer access is governed by Windows permissions and department procedure, not a separate role-based login system.
